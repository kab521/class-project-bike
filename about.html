Our Story

2026 — Idea Started

The idea for Wheelie Cool began after seeing a sportbike on campus and realizing how little beginner-friendly information existed online.

2026 — First Reviews

The site started with its first bike review, focusing on entry-level motorcycles and explaining terms like horsepower, CCs, and ergonomics.

2026 — Expanded Content

Added gear reviews, safety maps, and rider-focused guides to create a more complete resource for new riders.

Behind the Scenes

Wheelie Cool is built as a creative project combining design, storytelling, and real-world motorcycle culture.
